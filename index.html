Water insecurity means worry.

The WISE Scales make it visible.

A validated household scale that turns a hidden burden into a number researchers, NGOs, and governments can act on.

The questions of the WISE Scales help us bring the lived experience of water into focus. Here are a few of them:

WORRY

How often did you worry that you would not have enough water for all of your needs?

PLANS

How often did you have to change schedules or plans because of problems with water?

HANDS

How often were you not able to wash your hands after dirty activities because of problems with water?

DRINK

How often did you not have as much water to drink as you would have liked?

Global Impact

Water insecurity data for half the world

The WISE Scales improve the measurement of water-related issues faced by households and individuals. They help scientists, program developers, community leaders, and policymakers assess the extent of water insecurity, track changes over time, inform policy and program development, and evaluate intervention effectiveness in more than 70 countries.
